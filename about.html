OUR APPROACH

Professional service, with a local touch

At ML Mechanical Services, we know it can be difficult to decide what your vehicle needs when something goes wrong. We take time to listen, inspect the issue and explain the available options in plain language. You’ll have a chance to discuss the work and pricing before repairs begin.

Based at Woodmans Farm, Southampton, our workshop serves customers across Hampshire. From regular servicing to diagnostics and repairs, we work on cars and vans of many makes and models.

  • Clear explanation of findings and recommendations
  • Repair approval before work starts
  • Quality checks before vehicle collection
  • Local service for Southampton and nearby towns
